FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does it take to get set up?

Most angel networks are fully set up within two weeks. Some go live in a day. AgentOS handles the heavy lifting — connect your existing tools, set your investment thesis, and invite your angels.

Do I need to migrate my existing data?

No migration required. AgentOS connects to your existing stack in minutes — no disruption, no downtime. Just a platform that fits how you already work.

What's included in the agentic AI?

AgentOS's agentic AI automatically identifies the best angels in your network to champion a particular deal — based on their expertise, experience, and past investments. It also reads and categorizes incoming deals, screens them against your thesis, and flags anomalies.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts, no lock-in. Cancel anytime. The Starter plan is free forever — upgrade only when you're ready.

Is my data secure?

All data is encrypted both in transit and at rest using enterprise-grade AES-256 standards. Investor certifications, deal data, and member information are protected with role-based access controls and granular permissions.

Do you support Invest in Women and BBB reporting?

Yes — on the Growth and Enterprise plans. AgentOS automatically generates Invest in Women code reporting and British Business Bank metrics as part of your board pack. No spreadsheets required.
